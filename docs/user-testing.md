# Two-week user test

Before growing the library or adding features, find out whether the app helps, for whom and when. Nothing here needs analytics: the app keeps no usage data off the phone, so the test relies on what people tell you.

## Who

8–12 people, deliberately mixed: different ages, both languages, some who use other wellbeing apps and some who don't, and at least a few going through a harder stretch (busy job, exams, a change). Avoid only friends who'll be kind about it.

Tell them plainly: it's not a therapy app, and they can stop at any time.

## Setup

1. Install a development or TestFlight/internal build (widgets and reminders need a real build, not Expo Go).
2. Ask them to use it however they like for 14 days. Don't ask them to open it daily; whether they come back on their own is part of what you're learning.
3. Ask them to export a backup on day 14 (Settings › Your data) and send it to you **only if they're comfortable**. It contains saved messages, "Did this help?" answers, "not for me" marks and settings, never moods or free text. If they'd rather not, the interview is enough.

## Short check-ins (day 3 and day 8, five minutes, by message)

- When did you last open it, and what was going on?
- Did you pick something under "What would help right now?" Which one?

## Interview (day 14, 20–30 minutes)

The four questions that matter most:

1. **Which message helped, and why?** Ask them to find it (Saved or Settings › What has helped you).
2. **Which message made you feel misunderstood?** What did it get wrong?
3. **When did you come back without a reminder?** What made you think of the app?
4. **What was missing in that moment?**

Then, as time allows:

- Did you try one of the small steps (the "Try this · 30 seconds" card)? Did you finish it? Did it feel like a chore?
- Comfort, another view or a small step: which did you pick most? Did the message fit what you picked?
- Did you use "This one isn't for me"? Did the next message fit better?
- Reminders: full message or discreet? Why?
- Widget: did you add it? Did you read it?
- Would you pay for anything here? What, and how much? (Don't pitch A Little Better+; see what comes up.)
- If the app disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss, if anything?

## What to look for

- **A clear "who and when".** For example: "people with busy, anxious workdays, between meetings". That's your positioning, more than any feature.
- **Messages named as helpful vs. misunderstood.** Feed them into the next content pass (see [content-audit.md](content-audit.md)).
- **Return visits without a reminder.** A better signal than daily opens.
- **Small steps actually finished.** If almost nobody opens them, cut them back; if people love them, write more.

## The positioning question

Answer it in one sentence after the interviews, and keep it next to the roadmap:

> A Little Better is for ____________ when ____________, because it ____________ (and other apps don't).

Privacy alone won't fill the last blank: other apps also keep things private or have free cores. The test should show what does.
